## Summary
The project toolbar's buttons bind to `ActionId`s, and its enable and visibility rules move
out of `ProjectToolbar` into a new `ProjectToolbarPresenter`. Classic and neo look and
behave as before; the one intended change is that New project can no longer be clicked
twice while its dialog is open.

## Why
`ProjectToolbar` finds its buttons by string name: nine `WIDGET_NAME_*` constants
(`explorer/youngandroid/ProjectToolbar.java:28-36`) plus literals such as `"Folder"` and
`"Move"` (lines 86-89), 24 by-name calls in all. It also shows and hides neo-only buttons
(`"ImportProject"`, `"ImportTemplate"`, the `"Export"` menu button; lines 87-88, 92) that do
not exist in classic's template. `updateButtons()` reaches into the main menu through
`getTopToolbar().updateMenuState(…)` (lines 115, 123). `ProjectToolbarNeo` inherits all of
it and copies its two labels back to the parent (`style/neo/ProjectToolbarNeo.java:27-28`).
`enableStartButton()` (lines 130-134) re-enables New, but nothing on master disables it,
and `lockPublishButton` (line 44) is never read.

## Steps
1. Register each action with its `ActionId`, label and `IconKey` (framework from P3-09).
2. Switch the items in both templates to `action="…"` (neo's Export menu button to
   `EXPORT`) and drop every `name` attribute; keep each placement's own caption, tooltip,
   icon and style.
3. Move the rules from `updateButtons()`, `setProjectTabButtonsVisible()`,
   `setTrashTabButtonsVisible()`, the constructor (lines 56-62) and `enableStartButton()`
   into the presenter. Selection and read-only rules go on the actions (`setEnabled`,
   `setLabel`); gallery config goes on `LOGIN_TO_GALLERY` and `SEND_TO_GALLERY` (`setVisible`).
4. Which tab is shown (projects or trash) stays on the placements: the presenter calls
   `View.showTrash(boolean)` and each view shows or hides its own items and labels by
   `@UiField` reference. Do not hide the actions themselves: classic's File menu keeps New
   and Import while the trash is shown, and hiding `NEW_PROJECT` would remove them.
5. `ProjectToolbarNeo` implements `showTrash()` with its own fields instead of copying them
   to the parent.
6. Point `Ode.java:452-454` and `:475-477` at `showProjects()` / `showTrash()` and
   `ProjectList.java:309`, `:336` at `selectionChanged()`. Keep the
   `getTopToolbar().updateMenuState(…)` call inside the presenter until P3-11 removes it.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] No string widget names and no `setButton…`/`setDropDown…` calls remain in `ProjectToolbar*`.
- [ ] `updateButtons()`, `enableStartButton()` and both `set…TabButtonsVisible()` are gone;
      their rules live in `ProjectToolbarPresenter`, which imports no GWT widget.
- [ ] New project cannot be clicked twice: `NEW_PROJECT` is disabled while the wizard is open.
- [ ] Read-only accounts: New, Delete and Restore are disabled, as lines 111-116 do today.
- [ ] With more than one project selected, the export item reads "Export n selected projects"
      (`exportSelectedProjectsMenuItem`), otherwise `exportProjectMenuItem`.
- [ ] Login to Gallery shows only when the gallery is enabled; Send to Gallery also needs a
      gallery that is not read-only; both hide in the trash.
- [ ] Snapshot diff shows no change in classic or neo, light or dark.

## Rules
- At most ~400 added lines and ~20 files.
- No visual change besides the disabled New button while its dialog is open.
- Other toolbars and menus keep their `Command` children until their own ticket and P3-14.
- Short conventional commit subjects, e.g. `refactor(actions): project toolbar runs on
  action ids`; no AI attribution.

## How to verify
