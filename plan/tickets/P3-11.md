## Summary
The Projects ("File") and Build menus, and the keystore items, bind to `ActionId`s in both
layouts. The rules in `TopToolbar` that enable, hide and relabel them move into a new
`MainMenuPresenter`. Classic is unchanged. Neo gets two fixes it was meant to have: Download
Certificate Request hides like classic's, and the keystore items follow the keystore.

## Why
`TopToolbar` finds menu items by translated caption, e.g.
`fileDropDown.setItemEnabled(MESSAGES.saveMenuItem(), false)` (`TopToolbar.java:337`); 49 of
its 73 by-name calls are `setItemEnabled(caption, …)`. That breaks in three ways on master:
- Neo moved Download CSR and the three keystore items to its Settings menu
  (`style/neo/TopToolbarNeo.ui.xml:145-156`), but the code still looks in `fileDropDown`
  (`TopToolbar.java:129`, `:219-220`, `:396-437`). In neo the keystore items are never
  disabled, and Download CSR shows although iOS and App Store builds are off by default.
- Lines 331 and 359 target the Delete item through `deleteProjectButton()`, which equals
  `trashProjectMenuItem()` only in some locales (they differ in `es_ES`, `tr`, `pt`, `pt_BR`).
- Lines 193, 197, 206 and 333 toggle "Delete Forever", which no menu contains.
Neo is detected by menu name: `"ProjectDesignOnly".equals(fileDropDown.getName())` (lines
328, 356). `TopToolbarNeo` re-declares five `@UiField`s and copies them back
(`style/neo/TopToolbarNeo.java:44-49`).

## Steps
1. Register the actions (P3-09 pattern) and switch the 22 classic and 21 neo items to
   `action="…"`, dropping their `name`s and those of the menu buttons (`Project`,
   `ProjectDesignOnly`, `Build`). Keep each placement's caption and `visible="{…}"`.
2. Config, once at startup, on the actions: `DOWNLOAD_CSR` hidden unless iOS or App Store
   builds are allowed; `BUILD_APK_2`/`BUILD_AAB_2` need a second build server; the two iOS
   builds need `allowIosBuilds()` / `allowAppStoreBuilds()`; `GENERATE_YAIL` needs the Yail
   option and an admin. Then the view calls `removeUnneededSeparators()` as today; it deletes
   hidden items for good, so apply no other visibility before it.
3. View rules (skipped for read-only accounts, as line 319 does): in the designer, Save,
   Save as, Checkpoint, Project properties, Delete, Export and the builds are enabled; in the
   projects view they are disabled, Delete and Export follow the selection, and Export all
   needs one project. `DELETE_PROJECT` is hidden in the trash (today's `updateMoveToTrash`).
   Line 334's `projectCount == 0` rule is dropped: it only wins when
   `SwitchToProjectAction.java:16` runs it after `switchToProjectsView()`.
4. One owner per id: `MainMenuPresenter` owns `DELETE_PROJECT`, `EXPORT_PROJECT` (enabled and
   the "Export n selected projects" label) and `EXPORT_ALL_PROJECTS`. The project toolbar
   reports the selection to it instead of calling `updateMenuState()`, and stops writing them.
5. Keystore: keep the cache and pending flags; `DOWNLOAD_KEYSTORE` and `DELETE_KEYSTORE` are
   enabled when the keystore exists or the check fails.
6. Replace the `"ProjectDesignOnly"` check with `View.showProjectsMenu(boolean)`:
   `TopToolbarNeo` hides its menu in the projects view, classic ignores it. Remove the
   field copying from `TopToolbarNeo`.
7. Point the callers at the presenter (`Ode.getMainMenuPresenter()`) and delete the old methods.
