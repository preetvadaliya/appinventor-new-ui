## Summary
The generic property editors and panels in `widgets/properties/` build their controls from
kit pieces: text fields, checkboxes, menu buttons, trees, collapsible categories and
dialogs. The 48 component-specific editors that extend them inherit the change. Classic
and neo look and behave as before.

## Why
- `widgets/properties/` has 23 Java files. Seven construct raw widgets, 35 in total:
  `SubsetJSONPropertyEditor` 24 (8 `Button`, 7 `CheckBox`, 2 `Tree`, 1 `TreeItem`,
  2 `PopupPanel`, 1 `DropDownButton`, 1 `DialogBox`, 1 `TextBox`, 1 `FileUpload`),
  `AdditionalChoicePropertyEditor` 4 (lines 42, 70, 77, 96), `ChoicePropertyEditor` 3
  (`new DropDownButton(` at lines 122, 138, 188), `BooleanPropertyEditor` 1 (line 37),
  `ColorChoicePropertyEditor` 1 (`new TextButton()`, line 69), `TextPropertyEditor` 1
  (line 18), `TextAreaPropertyEditor` 1.
- Two more build GWT classes by inheritance: `PropertyHelpWidget.PropertyHelpPopup extends
  DialogBox` (line 38) and `CollapsibleCategoryPanel extends AbstractCollapsiblePanel`
  (line 17).
- `TextPropertyEditorBase` (200 lines) takes a GWT `TextBoxBase` in its constructor
  (line 52), so every text-like editor is tied to GWT's text box.
- 33 of the 48 editors in `editor/youngandroid/properties/` extend `ChoicePropertyEditor`,
  so converting it here converts them.

## Steps
1. `TextPropertyEditorBase` takes a kit `TextField` instead of `TextBoxBase`; adjust
   `TextPropertyEditor` and `TextAreaPropertyEditor` to pass `Ui.kit().textField()` with
   the right kind. Keep validation and the "commit on blur and Enter" behaviour.
2. `ChoicePropertyEditor`: replace the three `new DropDownButton(…)` with one
   `Ui.kit().menuButton()` filled from the choices; keep the selected caption and the
   triangle as today.
3. `BooleanPropertyEditor`, `AdditionalChoicePropertyEditor`, `ColorChoicePropertyEditor`:
   swap in `Checkbox`, `TextField`, `Button`.
4. `SubsetJSONPropertyEditor`: both trees become kit `Tree`s whose nodes hold kit
   `Checkbox`es; buttons and the save-as dialog come from the kit.
5. `PropertyHelpWidget`: the popup becomes a kit `Dialog`, non-modal and auto-hide as
   today. `CollapsibleCategoryPanel`: wrap a kit `Collapsible`.
