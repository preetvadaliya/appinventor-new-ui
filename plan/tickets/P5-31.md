## Summary
The App Inventor component property editors in `editor/youngandroid/properties/` build
their remaining raw controls (list boxes, buttons, radio buttons, text boxes, a checkbox,
the ListView data dialog) from kit pieces. Most editors need no change because they
inherit from the generic editors converted in P5-30. Classic and neo look and behave as
before.

## Why
- The folder has 54 files: 51 Java classes, `package-info.java` and 2 `.ui.xml`. 48 of the
  classes are editors (47 `*PropertyEditor` plus `YoungAndroidDataColumnSelectorProperty`);
  the others are `ListViewAddDataDialog`, `ListViewDataRow` and `ListWithNone`.
  (The plan's "52 editors" is off; use these numbers.)
- What the editors extend: `ChoicePropertyEditor` 33, `AdditionalChoicePropertyEditor` 6,
  `PropertyEditor` 3, `TextPropertyEditor` 2, and one each of `IntegerPropertyEditor`,
  `FloatPropertyEditor`, `ColorChoicePropertyEditor`, `BooleanPropertyEditor`. Those bases
  are done in P5-30, so 39 editors (the 33 choice editors plus the integer, float, color,
  boolean and two text editors) have nothing left to change.
- Raw widget constructions remain in 12 files (9 editors and the ListView dialog and row),
  30 in total:
  `YoungAndroidLengthPropertyEditor` 6 (4 `RadioButton` at lines 60-63, 2 `TextBox`),
  `ListViewAddDataDialog.ui.xml` 6 (`wiz:Dialog`, 5 `Button`, two of them `FocusTrap`),
  `ListViewDataRow.ui.xml` 3 (2 `TextBox`, 1 `Button`), `YoungAndroidGeoJSONPropertyEditor`
  3, `ListViewAddDataDialog.java` 2, `YoungAndroidAssetSelectorPropertyEditor` 2,
  `YoungAndroidFontTypefaceChoicePropertyEditor` 2, `YoungAndroidDefaultURLPropertyEditor`
  2, `YoungAndroidComponentSelectorPropertyEditor` 1, `YoungAndroidDataColumnSelectorProperty`
  1, `YoungAndroidListViewAddDataPropertyEditor` 1, `YoungAndroidSpriteOriginPropertyEditor` 1.
- Plus two inherited GWT popups: `ListViewAddDataDialog.Cancel extends DialogBox` (line 269)
  and `ListViewDataRow.AssetImagePicker extends PopupPanel` (line 282).
- Five list-based editors use `ListBox` in list mode (`setVisibleItemCount(10)`) through
  `ListWithNone.ListBoxWrapper` (`ListWithNone.java:19`).

## Steps
1. Let `ListWithNone.ListBoxWrapper` wrap a kit `Select`; keep the interface so
   `ListWithNoneTest`'s `FakeListBox` still works.
2. Swap each raw control for the kit piece listed above via `Ui.kit()`.
3. `YoungAndroidLengthPropertyEditor`: the four radios share one group name, passed to
   `Ui.kit().radio(group)`.
4. `ListViewAddDataDialog`: owner extends `KitView`; `<wiz:Dialog>` becomes
   `<k:Dialog>`; drop the `FocusTrap` buttons and their handlers; the `Cancel` inner
   `DialogBox` becomes a kit `Dialog`. `ListViewDataRow` uses `<k:TextField>` and
   `<k:Button>`.
