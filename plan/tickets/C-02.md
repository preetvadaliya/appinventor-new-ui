## Summary
MIT and the project agree one short window in which P7-38 and P7-39 are reviewed and
merged on the same day, and the window is announced in the umbrella issue from C-01 at
least a week ahead. No code changes.

## Why
P7-38 moves 150 files into `core/` and P7-39 about 309 into `features/` (counts from
`map.json`), with new package lines and imports. Any open contributor PR that touches one
of those files stops applying, and `git` follows a rename only when the file barely
changed. Landing both on one day, at a quiet time, gives contributors one rebase instead
of two, and lets the project help with it.

## Read first
- `docs/09-migration.md`
- `docs/decisions/0005-package-moves-last.md`
- `docs/10-pr-rules.md`

## In scope
- Proposing two or three candidate dates to MIT reviewers in the umbrella issue
- Listing open upstream PRs that touch `appengine/src/com/google/appinventor/client/`
  and pinging their authors
- A short rebase guide posted with the announcement

## Out of scope
- Writing P7-38 and P7-39 (they are prepared in advance and kept rebased)
- Any other phase's scheduling

## Steps
1. When P6-37 is merged, have P7-38 and P7-39 rebased, green and reviewed as drafts.
2. Propose dates in the umbrella issue, avoiding release weeks and school terms MIT names.
3. Once a date is agreed, list the open PRs that touch client files and comment on each
   with the date and the rebase guide.
4. On the day: merge P7-38, then P7-39, then post "done" in the umbrella issue.
5. Offer to rebase stuck contributor PRs for a week afterwards.

Rebase guide to post (short):

> After the move, rebase with `git rebase upstream/master`. If a file you changed moved,
> `git` usually follows it. If not, re-apply your change to the new path from
> `docs/file-map.html` (old path → new path), or ask in the umbrella issue and we will
> rebase it for you.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] A date is agreed with at least one MIT reviewer and posted in the umbrella issue.
- [ ] Authors of open client PRs were told at least a week before.
- [ ] P7-38 and P7-39 merged on that date, in that order.
- [ ] No contributor PR was left without an offer of help.

## Rules
- Do not merge P7-38 or P7-39 outside the agreed window.
- No AI attribution in issue comments.

## How to verify
- The umbrella issue shows the agreed date, the announcement and the "done" note.
