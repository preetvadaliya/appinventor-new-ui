## Summary
The Connect, Settings, Help and Admin menus and the top bar (account menu, top links, neo's
Help menu) bind to `ActionId`s in both layouts. Connection, settings and admin rules move into
`MainMenuPresenter`. The top bar's own logic (language list, read-only label, one-project
mode) stays in `TopPanel` for P4-19. Users see no change.

## Why
- Connect state is set by caption: `connectDropDown.setItemEnabled(MESSAGES.usbMenuItem(), …)`
  (`TopToolbar.java:223-246`), reached from JavaScript through `indicateDisconnect()`
  (`TopToolbar.java:252-255`, `editor/blocks/BlocklyPanel.java:322-323`).
- Autoload swaps label and command by id `"AutoloadLastProject"` in three places
  (`TopToolbar.java:165-168`, `actions/EnableAutoloadAction.java:24-26`,
  `actions/DisableAutoloadAction.java:24-26`); the dyslexic font item does the same at
  `TopToolbar.java:169-172`.
- Two lookups never match: `settingsDropDown.removeItem("AppStoreSettings")`
  (`TopToolbar.java:130`) compares with the caption "App Store Settings", and
  `accountButton.setItemVisible("DeleteAccount", false)` (`TopPanel.java:120`) with "Delete
  Account". App Store Settings always shows, and read-only users see Delete Account.
- Neo's delete-account item reuses `name="Signout"` (`style/neo/TopPanelNeo.ui.xml:72`, same
  as line 69), so it overwrites Sign out in `DropDownButton.itemsById`.
- Help is defined twice: classic in `TopToolbar.ui.xml:162-204` (12 items), neo in
  `style/neo/TopPanelNeo.ui.xml:26-62` (11 items). Nine are the same; classic adds Report an
  issue and the two companion items (neo has those under Connect); neo adds Guide and
  Feedback, which classic shows as top-bar buttons (`TopPanel.ui.xml:29-34`).
- `TopPanelNeo` copies ten fields back to `TopPanel` (`style/neo/TopPanelNeo.java:45-54`);
  its `myProjects` and `viewTrash` are only `ui:with` declarations (lines 14-15), never placed.

## Steps
1. Register the actions and switch all 61 placements (32 classic, 29 neo) to `action="…"`,
   dropping their `name`s and the menu buttons' (`ConnectTo`, `Settings`, `Help`, `Admin`).
   Keep captions and `dependentStyleName`s per placement.
2. Connection: `MainMenuPresenter.connectionChanged(boolean connected)` replaces
   `updateConnectToDropDownButton()`. Not connected: Companion enabled, then Chromebook or
   Emulator and USB, Refresh companion and Save to companion disabled; connected: the reverse.
   Nothing is set before the first call, as today. `indicateDisconnect()` keeps its JSNI name.
3. Settings: `TOGGLE_AUTOLOAD` and `TOGGLE_DYSLEXIC_FONT` take their label from the current
   setting and flip it; the autoload label changes after the settings save, as today.
4. Admin: hide `DOWNLOAD_USER_SOURCE` and `USER_ADMIN` and tell the view to drop the Admin
   menu for non-admins (`TopToolbar.java:174-176`).
5. Keep today's visible behaviour for the two broken lookups: App Store Settings and Delete
   account stay shown. Delete `TopToolbar.java:130`, `TopPanel.java:120` and
   `WIDGET_NAME_DELETE_ACCOUNT`, keeping the `else` branch that removes the read-only label.
6. Top bar: My Projects, Trash, Guide and Feedback (classic) and the account items (both)
   bind to actions; the one-project removal (`TopPanel.java:125-128`) and the language list
   (`SelectLanguage` items built in code) stay as they are.
7. Keep `getSettingsDropDown()` for the neo welcome popup (`Ode.java:1794`).

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] No caption or name lookups remain in `TopToolbar`, `TopPanel*` or the autoload actions.
- [ ] `EnableAutoloadAction`, `DisableAutoloadAction`, `SetFontRegularAction`,
      `SetFontDyslexicAction` are gone; their behaviour lives in the two toggle actions.
- [ ] Connect items enable and disable as before on connect, reset, hard reset and a
      disconnect reported by the companion; Chromebook items as before.
- [ ] Non-admins see no Admin menu; neo's Delete account no longer shares a name with Sign out.
- [ ] `MainMenuPresenter` imports no GWT widget.
- [ ] Snapshot diff shows no change in classic or neo, light or dark.
