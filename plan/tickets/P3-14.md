## Summary
Deletes the old path once every toolbar and menu runs on `ActionId`s: the by-name methods in
`Toolbar` and `DropDownButton`, item names, the `WIDGET_NAME_*` constants and the three parsers
that attach `Command` children. Users see no change.

## Why
On master 121 calls find a toolbar or menu item by name or caption. P3-10…P3-13 remove all
but six of them; this ticket removes the rest and the API itself, so the old way cannot
come back. The parsers come from a non-standard hook (`@ElementParserToUse`, from the
patched `UiBinderWriter` in `lib/gwt_svg/lib-gwt-svg-0.5.15.jar`). Three of them only turn a
child element into `setCommand(…)` and are dead once no template nests a `Command`. The other
two do more: `ToolbarParser` adds non-widget children such as `ToolbarItem` to a `Toolbar`
(`widgets/ToolbarParser.java:19-26`), and `DropDownButtonParser` adds items and turns `<hr/>`
into separators (`widgets/DropDownButtonParser.java:20-33`). GWT's own panel parser accepts
only widgets, so those two stay; P5-23 folds them into `controls/uibinder/ActionItemParser.java`,
as `docs/file-map.html` plans.
