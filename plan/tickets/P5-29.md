## Summary
The file, component, settings and system dialogs, and the dialogs built inline inside
four project-explorer commands and inside `MockComponent`, are built from controls. The
inline dialogs become their own classes under `features/dialogs/`, and the appearance
settings dialog gets a presenter. Classic and neo look and behave as before.

## Why
- Raw widget constructions in the in-scope files (Java `new X(` plus `.ui.xml` elements,
  plus `extends DialogBox`): `FileUploadWizard` 6, `FileUploadError` 4,
  `FileUploadErrorDialog` 3, `ComponentImportWizard` 2, `ComponentRenameWizard` 1,
  `ComponentUploadWizard` 1, `UISettingsWizard` 6 plus two raw `<input type="radio">`
  (`UISettingsWizard.ui.xml:22,34`), `AppStoreSettingsDialog` 7, `KeystoreUploadWizard` 1,
  `MarkOriginWizard` 1, `ErrorDialog` 3, `utils/MessageDialog` 3, `ProgressBarDialogBox` 2,
  `AddFormCommand` 4, `ShowBarcodeCommand` 4, `WarningDialogCommand` 3,
  `PreviewFileCommand` 6, `MockComponent` 10: 69 in total.
- Five of these extend `wizards/Wizard` (Component Import/Rename/Upload, Keystore,
  MarkOrigin) and inherit its 4 raw buttons each.
- Dialogs hidden inside non-UI classes: `AddFormCommand.NewFormDialog` (lines 74-286),
  `ShowBarcodeCommand.AppStoreDialogBox` (87) and `BarcodeDialogBox` (118),

## Steps
1. For each dialog: owner extends `SharedView`; `<wiz:Dialog>`/`DialogBox` becomes
   `Controls.active().dialog()` or `<controls:Dialog>`; buttons, text boxes, check boxes, list boxes and
   radios become `Button`, `TextField`, `Checkbox`, `Select`, `Radio`; `Wizard` subclasses
   use `Controls.active().wizard(…)`. Drop hand-made `FocusTrap` buttons.
2. Inline dialogs: move each inner class into its new file under `features/dialogs/`,
   taking only what it needs as constructor arguments or a small callback; the command or
   mock keeps the logic and calls the dialog.
3. `AppearancePresenter` owns the layout and theme choice (calls `ThemeService` from
   P2-08); `AppearanceDialog` implements `AppearancePresenter.View` with two `Radio`s
   and a `Select`.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] No `new Button(`, `new TextBox(`, `new CheckBox(`, `new ListBox(`, `new DialogBox(`,
      `extends DialogBox` or `LabeledTextBox` remains in the in-scope files.
- [ ] `explorer/commands/*Command.java` and `MockComponent.java` contain no dialog classes.
- [ ] Every dialog opens from the same menu or action as before, Escape closes it, Tab
      stays inside it, focus returns to the opener.
- [ ] Snapshot diff shows no change in classic or neo, light or dark.

## Rules
- At most ~400 added lines and ~20 files. This ticket is about 30 files and 600+ moved
  lines, so land it as four PRs: (a) files and components plus `PreviewFileDialog`,
  (b) settings and system (`AppearanceDialog`, AppStore, Keystore, MarkOrigin, ErrorDialog,
  MessageDialog, ProgressBarDialogBox), (c) `AddScreenDialog`, `BarcodeDialog`,
  `WarningDialog`, (d) the two `MockComponent` dialogs.
- No visual change. `features/` imports control interfaces only.
- Short conventional commit subjects, e.g. `refactor(dialogs): barcode dialog on the controls`;
  no AI attribution.
