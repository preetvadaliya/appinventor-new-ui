## Summary
Every project dialog (new project, new folder, move, import, templates, rename, download
source, no-projects welcome, project properties) is built from controls instead of raw
GWT widgets. The two new-project classes merge into one shared `NewProjectDialog` with a
`NewProjectPresenter`. Classic and neo look and behave as before.

## Why
- Raw widget constructions in the in-scope files (Java `new X(` plus `.ui.xml` elements):
  `NewYoungAndroidProjectWizard` 6, `NewFolderWizard` 7, `MoveProjectsWizard` 6,
  `ProjectUploadWizard` 4, `TemplateUploadWizard` 2, `InputTemplateUrlWizard` 1,
  `UrlImportWizard` 1, `RequestNewProjectNameWizard` 4, `DownloadUserSourceWizard` 2,
  `NoProjectDialogBox` 7, `ProjectPropertiesDialogBox` 5: 45 in total (27 `Button`, 6
  `LabeledTextBox`, 5 `wizards.Dialog`, 2 `Tree`, 2 `ListBox`, 1 `TextBox`, 1 `FileUpload`,
  1 `DialogBox`). Ten of the buttons are invisible `FocusTrap` buttons.
- Five of these classes extend `wizards/Wizard`, which adds 4 more raw buttons each.
- `wizards/NewProjectWizard.java` (78 lines) is an abstract `Wizard` that nothing extends;
  only its static `NewProjectCommand` and `createNewProject` are used
  (`NewYoungAndroidProjectWizard.java:186,202`, `Ode.java:59`,
  `NoProjectDialogBox.java:11`, `TemplateUploadWizard.java:20`, `LoadGalleryProject.java:22`).
- `RequestNewProjectNameWizard` extends `Wizard` yet builds its own `DialogBox` (line 56).

## Steps
1. Write `NewProjectPresenter` (no GWT widgets) and `NewProjectDialog extends SharedView
   implements NewProjectPresenter.View` with `<c:Dialog>`, `<c:TextField>`, `<c:Button>`.
   Point `NewProjectAction` and `NoProjectDialogBox` at `Layout.newProjectDialog()` via the
   presenter. Delete the two old classes.
2. Convert each remaining dialog: owner class extends `SharedView`; `<wiz:Dialog>` becomes
   `<c:Dialog>`, `<g:Button>` becomes `<c:Button>`, `<w:LabeledTextBox>` becomes
   `<c:TextField>`, `<g:Tree>` becomes `<c:Tree>`, `<g:ListBox>` becomes `<c:Select>`;
   Java `new Button(` becomes `Controls.active().button()` and so on. `Wizard` subclasses switch to
   `Controls.active().wizard(…)`.
3. Delete the `topInvisible`/`bottomInvisible` buttons and their `@UiHandler`s; the controls
   dialog's `FocusTrap` does that job.
4. `FolderTreeItem` becomes a `Tree.Node` user object instead of a `TreeItem` subclass.
5. `RequestNewProjectNameWizard`: drop the second `DialogBox` (line 56) and build the
   prompt as one controls dialog.

## Rules
- At most ~400 added lines and ~20 files. This ticket touches about 27 files, so land it

- `core/presenters/` imports no GWT widgets; `features/` imports control interfaces only.
- Short conventional commit subjects, e.g. `refactor(dialogs): new project dialog on the
  controls`; no AI attribution.
