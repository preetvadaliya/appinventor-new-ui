## Summary
The rest of the editor page becomes regions owned by each layout (decision 0009):
`ClassicViewer`, `ClassicMedia`, `ClassicProperties` (+ `ClassicPropertyRow` per property),
`ClassicBlockDrawers` and `ClassicBlocksWorkspace` in `layouts/classic/views/editor/`, and
the `Neo*` equivalents. Their logic lives once in `core/presenters/ViewerPresenter`,
`AssetsPresenter`, `PropertiesPresenter` and `BlockDrawersPresenter`. `ClassicDesignerArea`
and `ClassicBlocksArea` (P4-21) place these regions instead of the box widgets, so the
last designer `Box` singletons go. Classic and neo look and behave as before.

## Why
- `ViewerBox` (61 lines) hosts the whole `ProjectEditor` (`ViewerBox.show`,
  `ViewerBox.java:54-60`), so the form editor and the blocks editor share one box, and P4-21
  has to move it between the two areas.
- The viewer header: `style/neo/YaVisibleComponentsPanelNeo.java` (98 lines) extends
  `editor/youngandroid/YaVisibleComponentsPanel.java` (259 lines), re-declares 6 fields
  (`:24-29`), copies 4 back (`:93-96`) and copies the parent's `initializeListboxes`
  (`:37-73` vs `YaVisibleComponentsPanel.java:91-125`) only to toggle two icons. It is
  created through the factory hook P4-21 left on `Layout` (from
  `UiStyleFactory.createSimpleVisibleComponentsPanel`), reached through
  `ProjectEditor.getUiFactory()` (`ProjectEditor.java:108-110`) from `YaFormEditor.java:97`;
  `MockForm.java:1328-1357` casts to it.
- Assets: `explorer/youngandroid/AssetList.java` (218 lines) builds `new Tree()` (line 70),
  `new TextButton(` (77) and `new TreeItem(` (159); `explorer/project/ProjectNodeContextMenu.java:42`
  `new ContextMenu()`. Neo adds an "Assets" caption above it (`style/neo/Ode.ui.xml:34-37`).
- `PropertiesBox` (153 lines) and `widgets/properties/PropertiesPanel` (239) draw the
  property list; `BlockSelectorBox` (296 lines) draws the drawers with
  `SourceStructureExplorer`. `getAssetListBox()`, `getPropertiesBox()` and
  `getBlockSelectorBox()` are called 21 times in 5 files (`BlocksEditor` 12).
- `widgets/boxes/Box.java` (565 lines) has a caption button (`new TextButton(`, line 180)
  and a minimize/remove menu (`new ContextMenu()`, line 300) that never show: every
  subclass passes `minimizable=false, removable=false`.

## In scope
- New `core/presenters/ViewerPresenter.java` (preview size, phone preview, hidden
  components, non-visible components, the screen being designed), `AssetsPresenter.java`,
  `PropertiesPresenter.java` (categories, rows, one model per row from
  `PropertyEditors.forType`, P5-31), `BlockDrawersPresenter.java`.
- New per layout (`.java` + `.ui.xml`) in `views/editor/`: `<X>Viewer`, `<X>Media`,
  `<X>Properties`, `<X>PropertyRow`, `<X>BlockDrawers`, `<X>BlocksWorkspace`.
- `<X>DesignerArea` and `<X>BlocksArea`: place the regions; each area has its own `<X>Media`;
  the slots and re-parenting P4-21 added go.
- The viewer gets the current form's `MockForm` in its screen slot; the workspace gets the
  blocks editor's `BlocklyPanel` (shared, `features/`), instead of `ViewerBox` holding the
  `ProjectEditor` deck.
- Delete `ViewerBox`, `AssetListBox`, `PropertiesBox`, `BlockSelectorBox`,
  `YaVisibleComponentsPanel`, `YaVisibleComponentsPanelNeo`, `CollapsibleCategoryPanel`,
  the transitional `Layout.visibleComponentsPanel(…)`, the factory parameter of
  `ProjectEditor` (`:44, 66-69, 108-110`) and `YaProjectEditor` (`:218`), and the dead
  caption button and menu in `Box` (`ProjectListBox`, `AdminUserListBox` keep `Box`).

## Out of scope
- The editor views themselves (P5-30) and their logic (P5-31).
- Mock components and phone skins (shared, `features/`); Blockly's own drawing.
- The console, tutorial, data visualizer: they stay shared.
- Viewer, media, properties and drawer CSS (P6-33).

## Steps
1. Presenters from the logic in `YaVisibleComponentsPanel`, `SimpleNonVisibleComponentsPanel`,
   `AssetList`, `PropertiesBox`/`PropertiesPanel` and `BlockSelectorBox`; `View`s report
   events and draw. No widget in `core/`.
2. Templates as in the examples: `col pane` root, header, `scroll` body; lists Java fills
   are `FlowPanel`s; the phone screen and the Blockly workspace are `SimplePanel` slots;
   controls come from the layout. Keep today's style names next to the flex classes; neo
   keeps its icons and the "Assets" title.
3. Point `DesignerEditor`, `BlocksEditor`, `YaFormEditor`, `YaProjectEditor`, `MockForm`,
   `Ode` and `HTML5DragDrop` at the presenters.
4. `EditorPagePresenter.showMode` only shows one area; nothing moves between areas.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Twelve region views exist, each `<g:HTMLPanel>`-rooted; no neo class extends a
      classic one; no `super.x = x` remains in viewer views.
- [ ] The four presenters import no GWT widget class; their unit tests pass.
- [ ] The deleted classes above are gone; `grep -r "getViewerBox\|getAssetListBox\|getPropertiesBox\|getBlockSelectorBox" client/` is empty.
- [ ] Preview size, phone preview, "Display hidden components", asset upload and row menu,
      every property kind, and every block drawer behave as before.
- [ ] Snapshot diff shows no change in classic or neo, light or dark.

## Rules
- At most ~400 added lines and ~20 files: ship as `(1/2)` viewer, media and properties
  (designer area) and `(2/2)` block drawers and workspace (blocks area).
- No visual change. Templates follow decision 0008. A layout never imports another.
- Short conventional commit subjects, e.g. `refactor(designer): viewer is a layout
  region`; no AI attribution.

## How to verify

  property of each kind; open each built-in drawer and a component drawer; switch
  Designer/Blocks and screens; reload.
