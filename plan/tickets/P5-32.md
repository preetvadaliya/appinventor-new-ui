## Summary
The palette and the component tree become regions owned by each layout (decision 0009):
`ClassicPalette`, `ClassicPaletteItem` and `ClassicComponentTree` in
`layouts/classic/views/editor/`, `NeoPalette`, `NeoPaletteItem` and `NeoComponentTree` in
`layouts/neo/views/editor/`, built from that layout's controls. Their logic lives once in
`core/presenters/PalettePresenter` and `core/presenters/ComponentTreePresenter`, each with a
`View` the regions implement. `ClassicDesignerArea`/`NeoDesignerArea` (P4-21) place the
regions instead of `PaletteBox` and `SourceStructureBox`. Classic and neo look and behave
as before.

## Why
- The palette is one shared widget tree: `editor/simple/palette/AbstractPalettePanel.java`
  (589 lines) mixes search, category building and drag-and-drop with widget code; its
  search box is `new TextBox()` (line 157); `CollapsiblePaletteCategoryPanel extends
  AbstractCollapsiblePanel`; `ComponentHelpWidget.ComponentHelpPopup extends DialogBox`
  (line 39); items are `SimplePaletteItem` (228 lines). The five files in
  `editor/youngandroid/palette/` construct no widgets.
- The tree: `explorer/SourceStructureExplorer.java` (677 lines) subclasses `Tree` as
  `EventCaptureTree` (line 99) only to keep the last event for Shift/Ctrl selection, and
  builds Rename and Delete with `new TextButton(` (lines 356, 366);
  `boxes/SourceStructureBoxFilter.java:43` adds a `new DropDownButton(` with three
  `new DropDownItem(` (lines 39-41).
- Both sit in `Box` singletons: `PaletteBox` (42 lines, 7 `getPaletteBox()` calls in 6
  files) and `SourceStructureBox` (80 lines, plus `ISourceStructureBox`,
  `SourceStructureBoxClassic` and the filter; 9 `getSourceStructureBox()` calls in 4 files).
  Neo can only restyle them, never change their structure.

## In scope
- New `core/presenters/PalettePresenter.java` (search, categories, the component list from
  `SimpleComponentDatabase`, extension import, starting a drag) and
  `core/presenters/ComponentTreePresenter.java` (tree of the current screen, selection
  with Shift/Ctrl, rename, delete, the All / Visible / Non-visible filter).
- New per layout (`.java` + `.ui.xml`): `views/editor/<X>Palette`, `<X>PaletteItem`,
  `<X>ComponentTree`.
- `<X>DesignerArea.ui.xml`/`.java` (P4-21): the two regions replace the two boxes, and the
  area hands them to the presenters (no new `Layout` method; the area owns its regions).
- Callers of `getPaletteBox()`/`getSourceStructureBox()` (`DesignerEditor` 7, `Ode` 2,
  `BlocksEditor` 3, `MockComponent`, `YaFormEditor`, `HTML5DragDrop`) talk to the presenters.
- `ComponentHelpWidget`: the help popup becomes a shared `Dialog` (control interface).
- Delete `PaletteBox`, `SourceStructureBox`, `ISourceStructureBox`,
  `SourceStructureBoxClassic`, `SourceStructureBoxFilter`, `CollapsiblePaletteCategoryPanel`.

## Out of scope
- `ConsolePanel` (45 lines, a `FlowPanel` with a JSNI logger): stays shared in `features/`.
- The blocks drawers: `BlockSelectorBox` and `BlocksEditor` still build their tree with
  `SourceStructureExplorer` until P5-43; keep that class working for them.
- Viewer, media, properties, block drawers, blocks workspace (P5-43).
- Mock components on the canvas; palette and tree CSS (P6-33).

## Steps
1. Write the presenters from the logic in `AbstractPalettePanel`, `SourceStructureExplorer`
   and the filter; `View`s report events (search text, category toggled, item dragged or
   clicked, node selected with its modifier keys, rename, delete, filter chosen) and draw
   what the presenter says. No widget in `core/`.
2. Templates as in `docs/12-patterns.md`: a `flex-col pane` root, header, then a `flex-col grow overflow-auto` body;
   the palette's categories and results are `FlowPanel`s Java fills with `<X>PaletteItem`s;
   the tree is the layout's `Tree` control; buttons are the layout's `Button`s. Keep
   today's style names (`ode-PaletteCategory…`, `ode-SimplePaletteItem`) next to the flex
   classes and the `role="list"` inner panel.
3. Keep behaviour: the "/" shortcut that focuses search and its Return/Escape handlers
   (`AbstractPalettePanel.java:159-165, 179-193`); the selection event carries the
   `NativeEvent` that `MockDesignerRoot.java:208-210` reads Meta/Ctrl from.
4. Neo's regions keep neo's markup and icons; neither layout imports the other.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Six region views exist, each `<gwt:HTMLPanel>`-rooted; no class in `layouts/neo`
      extends one in `layouts/classic` or the reverse.
- [ ] `PalettePresenter` and `ComponentTreePresenter` import no GWT widget class; their
      unit tests (search filter, multi-select, filter modes) pass under `ant tests`.
- [ ] `PaletteBox` and the `SourceStructureBox*` classes are gone.
- [ ] Palette search, category open/close (mouse, Enter, Space), drag to the viewer and
      component help work; tree click, Shift+click, Ctrl/Cmd+click, Rename, Delete and the
      filter work.
- [ ] Snapshot diff shows no change in classic or neo, light or dark.

## Rules
- At most ~400 added lines and ~20 files: ship as `(1/2)` palette and `(2/2)` component
  tree, each with its presenter and both layouts' regions.
- No visual change. Templates follow decision 0008. A layout never imports another.
- Short conventional commit subjects, e.g. `refactor(designer): palette is a layout
  region`; no AI attribution.
