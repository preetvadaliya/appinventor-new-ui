## Summary
The designer's side panes build their controls from the kit: the palette's search field,
category panels and help popup, the component tree with its Rename and Delete buttons and
filter menu, the asset list with its tree, Upload button and row menu, and the pane frame
around them. Classic and neo look and behave as before.

## Why
Raw widget constructions and GWT subclasses in the in-scope files today:
- Palette: `editor/simple/palette/AbstractPalettePanel.java:157` `new TextBox()` (search);
  `CollapsiblePaletteCategoryPanel extends AbstractCollapsiblePanel`;
  `ComponentHelpWidget.ComponentHelpPopup extends DialogBox` (line 39). The five files in
  `editor/youngandroid/palette/` construct no widgets.
- Component tree: `explorer/SourceStructureExplorer.java:99` `EventCaptureTree extends
  Tree` only to keep the last event for Shift/Ctrl selection; `new TextButton(` at
  lines 356 and 366 (Rename, Delete); `boxes/SourceStructureBoxFilter.java:43`
  `new DropDownButton(` with three `new DropDownItem(` (lines 39-41).
- Assets: `explorer/youngandroid/AssetList.java:70` `new Tree()`, line 77
  `new TextButton(`, line 159 `new TreeItem(`; `explorer/project/ProjectNodeContextMenu.java:42`
  `new ContextMenu()` (the asset row menu; it lives in `explorer/project/`, not
  `explorer/youngandroid/`).
- Pane frame: `widgets/boxes/Box.java:180` `new TextButton(` and line 300
  `new ContextMenu()` (minimize/remove menu).
- Console: `editor/youngandroid/ConsolePanel.java` (45 lines) is a `FlowPanel` with an id
  and a JSNI logger; it has no buttons or inputs.
That is 13 constructions or subclasses, plus 3 menu items, across 8 files.

## Steps
1. Palette search: `Ui.kit().textField()` keeping the placeholder, the key-up, Return and
   Escape handlers (`AbstractPalettePanel.java:159-165`) and the "/" shortcut that focuses
   it (lines 179-193).
2. `CollapsiblePaletteCategoryPanel` wraps a kit `Collapsible`; keep its class names
   (`ode-PaletteCategory…`) and the `role="list"` inner panel.
3. `ComponentHelpWidget`: popup becomes a kit `Dialog`, auto-hide, positioned next to the
   help icon as today.
4. `SourceStructureExplorer`: replace `EventCaptureTree` with the kit `Tree`. The kit
   selection event must carry the triggering `NativeEvent`, which goes on to
   `SourceStructureExplorerItem.onSelected(NativeEvent)` (line 20) as `tree.lastEvent`
   does today (lines 165, 173); `MockDesignerRoot.java:208-210` reads Meta/Ctrl from it.
   Rename and Delete become kit `Button`s; if P3 gave them action ids, bind them with
   `setAction`.
5. `SourceStructureBoxFilter`: one kit `MenuButton` with All / Visible / Non-visible items.
6. `AssetList`: kit `Tree`; asset rows keep their icon and name; the Upload button and
   `ProjectNodeContextMenu` use kit pieces.
7. `Box`: control buttons and the pane menu come from the kit.
